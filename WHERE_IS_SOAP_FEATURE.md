## EXACT STEPS TO SEE "AI-Generated SOAP Summary"

### Step 1: Record Audio (or Upload File)
1. Click "Start Recording" button (purple button on right)
2. Allow microphone access if prompted
3. Say this test phrase: **"Patient has headache and fever, blood pressure is 120 over 80, diagnosed with flu, prescribed paracetamol"**
4. Click "Stop Recording" (red button)
5. **Result:** Audio file appears below with filename

### Step 2: Transcribe
1. Wait 2-3 seconds for language auto-detection
2. Language dropdown changes to "English"
3. **"Transcribe Audio" button becomes ENABLED (blue)**
4. Click "Transcribe Audio" button
5. Wait 3-5 seconds
6. **Result:** Transcription appears in a text box

### Step 3: Open Share Modal
1. Look below the transcription text box
2. You'll see buttons: **[Share]** [Copy] [Download]
3. **Click the purple "Share" button**
4. **Result:** Share modal opens

### Step 4: Find and Use SOAP
1. In the modal, look at **left column** "Content Options"
2. You'll see three checkboxes:
   - ☑ Original Transcription
   - ☐ English Translation
   - ☐ **AI-Generated SOAP Summary** ← THIS IS IT!
3. **Click the SOAP checkbox**
4. Wait 2-3 seconds (you'll see ⏳ spinner)
5. Look at **right column** "Preview"
6. **Result:** SOAP with S/O/A/P filled by AI appears!

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ "I'm looking on the main page"
**Fix:** SOAP is in the Share modal, not the main page

### ❌ "I don't see Share button"
**Fix:** Complete a transcription first

### ❌ "I see 'SOAP Summary Template'"
**Fix:** This means you're looking at old cached version. Hard refresh (Ctrl+F5)

### ❌ "I checked the box but nothing happens"
**Fix:** Click the "Generate Now" button if it appears
