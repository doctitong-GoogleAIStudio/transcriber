# How to Test AI-Generated SOAP Summary

## Prerequisites
The **Share** button and **AI-Generated SOAP Summary** feature only appear **AFTER** you complete a transcription.

## Step-by-Step Testing Guide

### Step 1: Complete a Transcription
1. Open the app: https://medical-transcribe-2.preview.emergentagent.com
2. **Option A - Upload Audio File:**
   - Click "Click to upload" in the Upload Audio File section
   - Select an audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A, etc.)
   
3. **Option B - Record Live:**
   - Click "Start Recording" button
   - Speak into your microphone (say something medical-related for better SOAP)
   - Click "Stop Recording" when done

4. Wait for language auto-detection (~2-3 seconds)
5. The language dropdown will auto-populate (e.g., "English")
6. Click the **"Transcribe Audio"** button
7. Wait for transcription to complete (~3-5 seconds)
8. You'll see the transcription result appear in a text box

### Step 2: Access the Share Feature
Once transcription is complete, you'll see action buttons:
- **Share** (purple gradient button) ← This is what you need!
- Copy
- Download
- Save Edits (if applicable)

### Step 3: Open Share Modal
1. Click the **Share** button
2. The "Share Transcription" modal will open

### Step 4: Generate AI-Powered SOAP
In the Share modal, you'll see:

**Left Column - Content Options:**
- ☑️ Original Transcription
- ☑️ English Translation (if available)
- ☑️ **AI-Generated SOAP Summary** ← Check this box!

**When you check the AI-Generated SOAP Summary box:**
1. A loading spinner appears next to the checkbox
2. You'll see "⏳ Generating AI-powered SOAP summary..." in the preview
3. After ~2-3 seconds, the AI-generated SOAP appears
4. If auto-generation doesn't trigger, click the **"Generate Now"** button

**Right Column - Preview:**
The preview will show:
```
File: your-audio-file.mp3
Language: English
Date: 3/20/2026, 12:45:00 PM
────────────────────────────────────────

TRANSCRIPTION:
[Your transcription text here]

SOAP SUMMARY:

S (Subjective): [AI-generated patient symptoms and complaints]

O (Objective): [AI-generated vital signs and clinical findings]

A (Assessment): [AI-generated diagnosis and clinical impression]

P (Plan): [AI-generated treatment plan and recommendations]

────────────────────────────────────────
Generated by Digos Doctors Hospital AI Audio Transcriber
© 2026 Digos Doctors Hospital
```

### Step 5: Verify AI Content
Check that all SOAP sections have **actual content** (not placeholders):
- ✅ S section should describe patient symptoms from transcription
- ✅ O section should list observable findings
- ✅ A section should provide clinical assessment
- ✅ P section should suggest treatment plan

### Step 6: Test Sharing Options
Once SOAP is generated, test the action buttons:
1. **Open Messenger** - Opens Facebook Messenger with content
2. **Copy Text** - Copies full preview to clipboard
3. **Download PDF/TXT** - Downloads formatted document

## Troubleshooting

### "Share button not visible"
**Cause:** No transcription completed yet
**Solution:** Complete steps 1-7 above first

### "AI-Generated SOAP Summary checkbox not visible"
**Cause:** Share modal not open
**Solution:** Click the purple "Share" button after transcription

### "SOAP shows placeholders [Add patient's description]"
**Cause:** SOAP generation didn't trigger
**Solution:** 
1. Click the "Generate Now" button (appears next to checkbox)
2. Check browser console (F12) for errors
3. Verify transcription contains medical content

### "Generate Now button not visible"
**Cause:** One of these conditions:
- SOAP is currently generating (wait 2-3 seconds)
- SOAP already generated successfully
- Checkbox is unchecked

**Solution:** Uncheck and re-check the SOAP checkbox

### "Loading spinner stuck"
**Cause:** API timeout or error
**Solution:**
1. Wait 5 seconds
2. Uncheck and re-check SOAP checkbox
3. Check browser console (F12) for error messages
4. Try with shorter transcription

## Test Transcription Examples

### Example 1: Simple Medical Case
**What to say/transcribe:**
"Patient complains of headache for two days. Blood pressure is 120 over 80. Temperature is normal at 36.5 degrees Celsius. No fever. Diagnosed with tension headache. Prescribed ibuprofen 400mg three times daily."

**Expected SOAP:**
- S: Headache for 2 days
- O: BP 120/80, Temp 36.5°C, afebrile
- A: Tension headache
- P: Ibuprofen 400mg TID

### Example 2: Detailed Consultation
**What to say/transcribe:**
"The patient is a 45 year old male presenting with chest pain that started this morning. Pain is sharp, 7 out of 10 intensity, radiating to left arm. Patient has history of hypertension. On examination, blood pressure is elevated at 150 over 95. Heart rate is 88 beats per minute. Respiratory rate is normal. Lungs are clear. Heart sounds normal with no murmurs. ECG shows no acute changes. Likely angina pectoris. Plan is to start aspirin, refer to cardiologist, and order stress test."

**Expected SOAP:**
- S: Chest pain, sharp, 7/10, radiating to left arm, started today, HTN history
- O: BP 150/95, HR 88, RR normal, lungs clear, heart sounds normal, ECG no acute changes
- A: Angina pectoris
- P: Start aspirin, cardiology referral, stress test ordered

## Debug Mode

To see detailed logs:
1. Open browser console (F12)
2. Go to "Console" tab
3. Look for these log messages:
   - 🔍 SOAP useEffect check
   - 🚀 Triggering SOAP generation
   - 📤 Sending request to /api/generate-soap
   - ✅ SOAP generated successfully
   - 📋 Generating preview
   - ✅ Using generated SOAP summary

## Contact Support

If SOAP generation still shows placeholders after following all steps:
1. Take screenshot of Share modal
2. Copy browser console logs (F12 → Console tab)
3. Note the transcription text used
4. Report issue with above information
